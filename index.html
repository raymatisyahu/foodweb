Food Web Roles: Producers, Consumers, Scavengers, and Decomposers

1. Producers

Plants make their own food using sunlight. They are the beginning of most food chains.

☀️ Sun → 🌱 Plant

2. Consumers

Consumers get energy by eating plants or other animals.

🌱 Grass → 🐇 Rabbit → 🦅 Eagle

The arrow shows the direction of energy transfer.

3. Decomposers

Decomposers, such as mushrooms, break down dead organisms and return nutrients to the soil.

🍂 Dead matter → 🍄 Mushroom → 🌱 Plant

4. Food Web

A food web is made of connected food chains.

🌱 Plant → 🐇 Rabbit → 🦅 Eagle
🌱 Plant → 🐛 Caterpillar → 🐦 Bird
🐛 Caterpillar → 🐸 Frog → 🦅 Eagle

Remember ⭐

Producer = makes food • Consumer = eats organisms • Decomposer = breaks down dead matter
